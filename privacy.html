Privacy Policy: Under the Prompt poster

Last updated: 14 October 2026.

"Under the Prompt poster" is a private tool used by one person, the owner of the Under the Prompt channel, to upload their own finished videos to their own YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook accounts. It is not offered to the public.

What it uses

  • The access tokens each platform issues when the owner signs in, used only to upload the owner's videos and read the resulting post IDs and URLs.
  • Basic profile information (account name and ID) to confirm the right account is signed in.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect, sell or share data about anyone else.
  • It does not read other users' content, messages or followers.

Storage and deletion

Tokens are stored only on the owner's computer and can be revoked at any time from each platform's settings (Google account permissions, TikTok "Manage app permissions", Facebook "Business integrations"). To ask about this policy, contact the channel through YouTube: https://www.youtube.com/@underthepromptyt.
