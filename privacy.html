Privacy Policy: Under the Prompt poster

Last updated: 14 October 2026.

"Under the Prompt poster" (the "app") is a private tool used by one person, the owner of the Under the Prompt channel, to upload their own finished videos to their own YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook accounts. It is not offered to the public. By signing in to the app you agree to this policy.

Platforms and APIs used

  • YouTube: the app uses the YouTube API Services. Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy (http://www.google.com/policies/privacy). Use of YouTube through the app is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service.
  • TikTok: the TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API.
  • Instagram and Facebook: the Meta Graph API (Instagram content publishing and Facebook Pages).

What information the app accesses, collects, stores and uses

  • Sign-in tokens issued by each platform when the owner signs in. Used only to upload the owner's videos and set their title, description, tags, thumbnail and privacy setting.
  • Basic account details (account or channel name and ID), used only to confirm the right account is signed in.
  • Post details returned after upload (post ID, URL, upload time), stored so the owner can track their own posts and avoid posting twice.

The app does not access anyone else's data: no other users' videos, comments, messages, followers or analytics.

How the information is used and shared

The information is used only to publish the owner's own videos and keep a record of those posts. It is not sold, shared with, or disclosed to any internal or external party, and it is not used for advertising. The app shows no ads and no third-party content.

Storage on the device

All tokens and post records are stored only on the owner's own computer, in files readable only by the owner. The app uses no cookies and runs no website tracking. Data obtained from the platforms (other than tokens and the owner's own post IDs) is not kept for more than 30 days.

Revoking access

  • Google / YouTube: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions.
  • Facebook and Instagram: Facebook Settings → Apps and websites (or Business integrations); Instagram Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites.

When access is revoked, the app's stored tokens stop working and are deleted, along with any related data, within 7 days.

Data deletion

To have the app's stored data about your account deleted, revoke access as shown above and open a request at github.com/lourens0312-prog/undertheprompt/issues (no personal details needed, just the platform name). The data is deleted within 7 days. This does not delete content you posted on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram or Facebook; delete that on the platform itself.

Contact

For privacy questions or complaints, open an issue at github.com/lourens0312-prog/undertheprompt/issues or message the channel at youtube.com/@underthepromptyt.
